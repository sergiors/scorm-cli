# Make audio and video understandable

Captions show spoken dialogue and meaningful sounds in synchronized text. They help people who are Deaf or hard of hearing, and can also help in noisy or quiet settings.

Review captions for accuracy, speaker changes, timing, and relevant sounds. Automatic captions can be a starting point, but they often need correction.

A transcript provides the media's spoken content as text. It is useful when someone cannot play the media or prefers to read, but it does not replace synchronized captions when captions are needed.
