# Test without a mouse

Some people navigate with a keyboard, switch, or voice control. Check that every interactive control can be reached and used without a mouse.

1. Use `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` to move through controls in a sensible order.
2. Use the expected key, such as `Enter` or `Space`, to activate a control.
3. Confirm that the current focus is visibly indicated.
4. Check that dialogs and menus do not trap focus or hide it when they close.

Prefer native links and buttons: browsers already provide familiar keyboard behavior for them. Avoid removing the focus indicator unless you replace it with one that is at least as visible.
