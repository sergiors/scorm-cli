# Add images with context

Local image paths must resolve to assets inside the content package, though the parser also accepts absolute remote asset URLs. `<Image>` requires explicit alternative text.

## Image: asset source, required alt text

`<Image>` can be authored as a block or inline with paragraph text. Its required attributes are `src` and `alt`; `caption` is optional. Keep the alternative concise and useful for someone who cannot see the image.

<Image
  src="../assets/course-landscape.jpg"
  alt="A mountain landscape beneath a wide, open sky"
  caption="A landscape photo bundled locally with this course."
/>

Here the same locally bundled image can sit in a sentence: <Image src="../assets/course-landscape.jpg" alt="A mountain landscape beneath a wide, open sky" /> illustrates the inline form.

<Callout variant="info" title="Make images usable">
  Provide meaningful image alternatives.
</Callout>
