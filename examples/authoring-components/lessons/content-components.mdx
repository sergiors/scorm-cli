# Content blocks and nested structure

Components such as `<Callout>` and `<Accordion>` are flow blocks: place them in lesson content where a block belongs, not at the course root. Their bodies accept lesson content, but the accordion's own structural children are deliberately limited.

## Emphasize a message with Callout

`<Callout>` wraps Markdown content. `variant` may be `note` (the default), `info`, `tip`, `warning`, or `danger`; the optional `title` is a non-empty string.

<Callout variant="tip" title="Use the component for its meaning">
  A note adds context, a warning flags a likely problem, and a danger callout emphasizes a serious
  risk. The variants change presentation, not the allowed content grammar.
</Callout>

The body can include ordinary Markdown blocks and other valid flow components. For example, this warning contains a list:

<Callout variant="warning">
  Before publishing, check that each lesson has a useful title and that every media asset has an
  appropriate alternative.

Keep the message focused, and put supporting detail in the body.

  </Callout>

## Group related detail with Accordion

An `<Accordion>` contains one or more direct `<Item>` children. Every accordion `Item` must have exactly one direct `<Title>` and one direct `<Content>`. The title supports normal inline Markdown; the content accepts flow content.

`multiple` allows several sections to be open at once. `collapsible` lets the only open section close. These flags cannot both be true. Each `Item` can set the boolean `defaultOpen`; a single-open accordion can have at most one such item.

<Accordion multiple>
  <Item defaultOpen>
    <Title>What belongs in a title?</Title>
    <Content>
      Keep it short and descriptive. Inline Markdown such as **bold text** is supported.
    </Content>
  </Item>
  <Item>
    <Title>What belongs in Content?</Title>
    <Content>
      Content can hold paragraphs, lists, callouts, media, and other supported flow blocks.

      <Callout variant="note">The Title and Content must remain direct children of their Item.</Callout>
    </Content>

  </Item>
</Accordion>

The names `<Item>`, `<Title>`, and `<Content>` are structural markers, not standalone flow components. Their meaning comes from their direct position inside `<Accordion>` and its `<Item>`.
